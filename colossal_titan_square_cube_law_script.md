## Narration Script

### Hook
> "Kya Attack on Titan ka 60-meter lamba Colossal Titan real life mein zinda reh sakta hai?"

### Body
> "Anime mein toh Titans bina kisi problem ke move kar lete hain. Lekin science ke mutaabiq, jab koi creature bada hota hai, toh uska volume and weight cube ki speed se badhta hai, jabki bones aur muscles sirf square ki speed se! Ise kehte hain Square-Cube Law. Is law ke chalte, itne bade weight ke neeche move karte hi haddiyon par bahut zyada stress padega, aur Titan ka apna weight sambhalna mushkil ho jayega!"

### Takeaway
> "Toh अगली baar anime dekho, toh physics ke is rule ko zaroor yaad rakhna!"

## Research References & Academic Citations

1. **Claim:** As linear dimensions increase, surface area scales quadratically while volume and mass scale cubically, limiting the maximum size of weight-bearing structures.
   - **Source Title:** *On Growth and Form* (D'Arcy Thompson)
   - **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781107325852
   - **Verification Status:** Verified (Cambridge University Press)

2. **Claim:** Scaling limits in land animals dictate that skeletal mass must make up an increasingly disproportionate percentage of total body mass as size increases.
   - **Source Title:** *Scaling: Why Animal Size is So Important* (Knut Schmidt-Nielsen)
   - **DOI / URL:** https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139167826
   - **Verification Status:** Verified (Cambridge University Press)
